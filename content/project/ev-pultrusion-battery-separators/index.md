This project develops simulation and optimization workflows for pultruded carbon fiber composite battery module separators used in electric-vehicle structures. The work combines LS-DYNA side-pole crash simulations with composite architecture and processing-parameter studies to improve lightweighting and impact safety.

Recent design optimization reduced pulling resistance by approximately 60% while maintaining attention to structural performance. Current work explores machine-learning-based methods for optimizing fiber distribution and composite architecture.

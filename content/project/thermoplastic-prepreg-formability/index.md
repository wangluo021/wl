This project studied the thermomechanical behavior and formability of aerospace thermoplastic prepregs. The work characterized viscoelastic response through DMA stress relaxation and thermal ramp tests, fabricated thin-ply composite laminates by hot pressing, and evaluated bending behavior through DMA-based four-point bending experiments.

The project supports process understanding for thermoplastic composite forming by linking temperature-dependent material response with manufacturability and deformation behavior.

This project evaluated robot-based additive manufacturing of modular molds for wind blade composite fabrication. The work included temperature profiling with thermocouples, vacuum-integrity testing, and surface-deformation evaluation across printed mold sections.

The workflow supported glass fiber/epoxy composite fabrication using 3D-printed molds, maintaining vacuum levels below approximately 10 kPa and reducing surface deviation to within approximately 0.5 mm.
